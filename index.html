Why two views? The Total Dump labels every delivered product with an L1 like "Jam & Spreads" or "Honey & Chyawanprash" — these are the consumer-facing product categories. The Campaign Mapper labels every ad campaign with a category like Jam, Honey, Branded, or Fruit Mines — these are the marketing buckets. They overlap but aren't identical: e.g. "Branded" is a marketing strategy (defending brand-name keywords) that doesn't appear as a product L1, and "Energy Bars" sells organically but has no dedicated ad budget.
